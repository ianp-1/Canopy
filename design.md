# Design System: AgriSure
**Project ID:** 12338449975890287120

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The AgriSure design language is **Cinematic, Data-Rich, and Trustworthy**. It combines the density of a complex DeFi protocol with the expansive imagery of modern agriculture. The "vibe" is **Parametric Precision**—everything feels calculated, transparent, and instant.
-   **Mood:** High-tech, nocturnal (deep field at night), illuminated by data.
-   **Density:** Rich information density in dashboards, balanced by expansive negative space in landing areas.
-   **Aesthetic:** Glassmorphism over dark, organic textures (forest greens) creates a sense of depth and modernity.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
*   **Neon Cyber Lime (#11d452):** Used for primary actions (CTAs), positive data trends, and active statuses. It represents "growth" and "liquidity".
*   **Deep Forest Void (#0a2518):** The primary background color. A deep, rich green-black that provides high contrast for the neon accents without the harshness of pure black.
*   **Abyssal Black (#050505):** Used for header backgrounds, footer backgrounds, and deep structural layers.
*   **Crystalline Glass (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)):** Used for secondary containers, cards, and panels. Features a background blur to create separation.
*   **Signal White (#FFFFFF):** Used for primary headings and critical text for maximum legibility.
*   **Muted Stardust (#94A3B8):** Used for secondary text, labels, and descriptions.

## 3. Typography Rules
**Font Family:** `Space Grotesk`
*   **Headings:** Used for all section titles and key metrics. Space Grotesk's technical geometry reinforces the "parametric/algorithmic" nature of the protocol.
    *   *Usage:* Bold (700) or Medium (500) for hierarchy.
*   **Body:** Used for interface text, descriptions, and data labels.
    *   *Usage:* Regular (400) for readability. Letter-spacing is slightly open to enhance technical feel.

## 4. Component Stylings
*   **Buttons:**
    *   *Primary:* Solid Neon Cyber Lime (#11d452) with contrasting dark text. Shape is `rounded-lg` (Round Eight). High glow effect on hover.
    *   *Secondary:* Glass background with White outline. Subtle hover state.
*   **Cards & Containers:**
    *   *Style:* Glassmorphism. Translucent backgrounds with a `backdrop-blur`.
    *   *Borders:* Thin, subtle borders (1px) in white/alpha (e.g., `border-white/10`) to define edges without heavy lines.
    *   *Shadows:* "Whisper-soft" diffused shadows to lift cards off the dark background.
    *   *Corners:* `rounded-lg` (8px radius) for a modern, friendly but precise look.
*   **Inputs & Widgets:**
    *   *Fields:* Darker background (`bg-black/20`) with neon focus states.
    *   *Charts:* Neon gradients (Stroke #11d452 fading to transparent) on dark grids.

## 5. Layout Principles
*   **Grid System:** Strict alignment for data visualizations to maintain legibility.
*   **Whitespace:** "Cinematic" spacing. Large margins between sections on the landing page to let the imagery breathe. Tighter, functional density in the dashboard.
*   **Z-Index Layering:** Background (Imagery) -> Layer 1 (Glass Panes) -> Layer 2 (Neon Highlights/Text).
